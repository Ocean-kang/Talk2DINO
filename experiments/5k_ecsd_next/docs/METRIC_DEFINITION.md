# Metric Definitions

## 1. CISD

For five L2-normalized CLIP text embeddings \(z_1,\ldots,z_5\):

\[
CISD(S)=1-\frac{1}{10}\sum_{i<j}\cos(z_i,z_j)
\]

There are ten unordered pairs. Higher CISD means lower average pairwise similarity and therefore greater text-embedding dispersion. It does not directly measure semantic coverage or visual correctness.

## 2. Semantic fact coverage

Let \(F_{source}\) be the union of normalized content tokens from the five Raw Captions. Let \(F_{covered}\) be the union of content tokens from the selected five captions.

Each source fact receives inverse-frequency weight:

\[
w_t=1+\log\left(\frac{N+1}{freq(t)+1}\right)
\]

where \(N=5\) is the number of Raw Captions and `freq(t)` counts Raw Caption occurrences.

\[
Coverage(S)=
\frac{\sum_{t\in F_{covered}\cap F_{source}}w_t}
{\sum_{t\in F_{source}}w_t}
\]

This is a lexical evidence-coverage proxy. It is not an ontology, object detector, or human-verified concept recall metric.

## 3. Greedy incremental coverage

\[
\Delta Coverage(c|S)=
\frac{\sum_{t\in F(c)\setminus F(S)}w_t}
{\sum_{t\in F_{source}}w_t}
\]

The value is in [0,1] when candidate facts are drawn from the source evidence.

## 4. CLIP diversity

For candidate `c` and selected set `S`:

\[
D(c|S)=
\frac{1}{|S|}\sum_{s\in S}\frac{1-\cos(z_c,z_s)}{2}
\]

CLIP embeddings are generated by the same cached ViT-B/16 encoder for all methods.

## 5. Semantic role novelty

For known role set `Roles(c)`:

\[
R(c|S)=
\frac{|Roles(c)\setminus Roles(S)|}{|Roles(c)|}
\]

The known role inventory is Entity, Attribute, Action, Relation, and Context. Unknown is excluded. If no known role is assigned, novelty is zero.

## 6. Mean pairwise similarity and redundancy

Mean pairwise similarity is the mean CLIP cosine similarity across the ten caption pairs.

Redundancy is the mean weighted Jaccard overlap across the ten fact-set pairs:

\[
J_w(A,B)=
\frac{\sum_{t\in A\cap B}w_t}
{\sum_{t\in A\cup B}w_t}
\]

CISD and semantic coverage are reported independently. Redundancy is a diagnostic, not a term in the B1-B4 objective.

## 7. Category coverage

The rule-based classifier assigns multi-label categories. Per-image category coverage is:

\[
CategoryCoverage(S)=\frac{|\bigcup_{c\in S}Roles(c)|}{5}
\]

Unknown is excluded from the numerator. This metric measures lexical-role coverage under the fixed rule inventory.

## 8. Candidate quality proxies

- Invalid ratio: candidates with one or more surface-form warnings divided by candidate count.
- Duplicate ratio: normalized candidate strings repeated within an image divided by candidate count.
- Unsupported-content ratio: candidates containing content tokens absent from the original Raw Captions divided by candidate count.
- Candidate validity rate: selected captions without automatic surface-form warnings divided by five.

These automatic measures must not be presented as human-verified correctness. Use the 100-image annotation set to estimate human validity, unsupported-content, completeness, category accuracy, and redundancy.

## 9. Multi-seed statistics

For mIoU values \(m_1,m_2,m_3\):

\[
Mean=\frac{1}{3}\sum_i m_i
\]

\[
Std_{pop}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{3}\sum_i(m_i-Mean)^2}
\]

The package reports population standard deviation, best, worst, and relative differences. Three seeds are not sufficient for a strong statistical-significance claim.
